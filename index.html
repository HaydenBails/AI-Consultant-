Questions

Fair questions before you spend $400

Why is it only $400?

Because it's a productized process, not custom consulting. A structured questionnaire, a consistent analysis, and a templated report mean the audit doesn't carry the cost of a bespoke engagement. It's also how we start: if the automations are worth building, some clients go on to the $1,500 implementation. The audit is designed to stand on its own either way, which is why it's backed by the guarantee.

What if I'm not technical?

You don't need to be. The questionnaire asks how your agency works — not what software you should buy. The report is written in plain language, and every recommendation explains what it does and why before it ever names a tool. You can read it, understand it, and decide what to do with it without a technical background.

Do you build it too?

Optionally, yes. Every report's top recommendation comes with a $1,500 fixed-price build and a timeline. There's no obligation and no retainer — plenty of the roadmap is simple enough to hand to whoever already helps you with your tools. The audit's job is to tell you what's worth doing; building it is your call.

What tools do you use?

Automations are built on n8n (an open-source workflow tool), the Claude API for anything that involves reading or writing text, and the systems you already run — your ATS, email, calendar, and spreadsheets. We don't ask you to switch platforms; the goal is to remove manual steps between the tools you already have.

Is my data confidential?

Yes. Your answers are used only to produce your audit. We don't share, sell, or publish anything about your business. The sample report on this page is fictional precisely so we never have to show a real client's operations to make a point.
