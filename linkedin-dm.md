# LinkedIn Outreach — Recruiting Agencies

For prospects where email is Low-confidence (see `icp.md`) or where LinkedIn is
simply the better channel because the owner is active there. Two pieces: a
connection note, then a follow-up DM (the LinkedIn variant of cold-email touch 1).

Placeholders: `{{FOUNDER_NAME}}`, `{{SAMPLE_REPORT_LINK}}`, `{{CALENDAR_LINK}}`,
`{{LANDING_PAGE_URL}}`.

Same rules as email: honest about being new, no invented clients or results, no
fake scarcity, one clear CTA. The sample report is fictional and labeled as such.

---

## 1. Connection note (under 300 characters)

**Personalization slot `[[specific detail]]`** — 2-min recipe: one true thing from
their profile or company — their vertical, a recent post, or open-role count
(e.g. "your software recruiting desk" or "the coordinator role you're hiring").

> Hi [First name] — I build automations for small recruiting agencies to cut the
> admin (candidate re-keying, submittal formatting, scheduling). [[specific
> detail]] is why I wanted to connect. Not pitching here — happy to share a sample
> audit if it's useful.

*(Character count with a short detail lands ~270; trim the detail if over 300.)*

**Why this works:** it names the value, references something real, and explicitly
says "not pitching," which lifts accept rates. No ask yet.

---

## 2. Follow-up DM (send after they accept) — LinkedIn variant of touch 1

**Personalization slot `[[their vertical / drag signal]]`** — 2-min recipe: reuse
the note from your sheet's personalization column — their niche or the specific
drag signal you spotted.

> Thanks for connecting, [First name].
>
> Quick context on what I do: a $400 AI Operations Audit for recruiting agencies.
> I map your workflow and send back a report in 48 hours showing what to automate,
> the hours and dollars it saves, and a 90-day plan. If it doesn't find at least
> 10 hours a month worth recovering, it's free.
>
> I mentioned [[their vertical / drag signal]] — that's usually where the manual
> work stacks up for an agency your size.
>
> Here's a full sample (a fictional demo agency, clearly labeled):
> {{SAMPLE_REPORT_LINK}}
>
> Worth a 20-minute call to see if it fits? I can send a couple of times, or grab
> a slot here: {{CALENDAR_LINK}}

**Honesty note:** I'm newly launched — no client roster to name-drop yet. The
sample shows the depth and format of the work; the guarantee is there so trying it
is genuinely low-risk for you.

---

## Sequencing on LinkedIn
- **Day 0:** send the connection note.
- **On accept:** send the follow-up DM (don't wait for a scheduled day; strike
  while the accept is fresh).
- **Day +3 after DM, no reply:** one short nudge — "No rush, [First name] — want me
  to send the sample over, or is timing off right now?"
- **Day +7:** leave it. Don't send a third DM; it reads as pressure on LinkedIn.
- If they engage, move to the call (see `call-script.md`).

Keep total LinkedIn touches per prospect to connection + two messages max.
