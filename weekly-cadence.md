# Weekly Cadence — 25 Personalized Touches/Day

A Mon–Fri operating rhythm for the first two weeks: hit **25 personalized touches
per day** across email, LinkedIn, and Upwork, and work the funnel toward **3
booked audits in ~2 weeks**. Honest math below — the target is achievable but
aggressive and depends on list quality and genuine personalization.

A "touch" = one personalized message actually sent (a first email, a follow-up, a
LinkedIn note/DM, or an Upwork proposal). Follow-ups count.

---

## Daily channel mix (25 touches)

| Channel | Touches/day | Notes |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| Cold email | 12 | New sends + Day-3 / Day-7 follow-ups on the same threads |
| LinkedIn | 10 | Connection notes + accepted-DM follow-ups + light nudges |
| Upwork | 3 | Only when fitting posts exist; if none, roll the 3 into email |

As the sequence matures (from ~Day 4 on), a growing share of the 25 are
**follow-ups** to people already contacted — that's expected and fine. Fresh
first-touches taper as the 100-prospect list gets worked.

---

## Daily time blocks (~2.5–3 focused hours)

| Time | Block | What you do |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Prep (night before) | 15 min | Pull tomorrow's prospects from the sheet; write their personalization notes (column N) so morning is just sending |
| Morning 1 | 45 min | **Email:** 12 personalized touches (Tue–Thu mornings convert best) |
| Morning 2 | 40 min | **LinkedIn:** 10 touches — connection notes to new prospects, DMs to accepts, nudges |
| Midday | 30 min | **Upwork:** scan new posts, send 3 tailored proposals with post-referencing hooks |
| Afternoon | 20 min | **Inbox + booking:** reply to responders within the hour, book calls, update the sheet |
| End of day | 10 min | Update Status/Reply/Next-action columns; note what to send tomorrow |

**Guardrails:** LinkedIn — keep new connection requests to ~10–15/day to avoid
limits. Email — warm the domain; if sending 12+/day cold from a new domain,
consider a dedicated sending domain and keep volume steady, not spiky.

## The funnel math (be honest with yourself)

Over 2 weeks you fully sequence ~**100 prospects** (25 touches/day × 10 days ≈ 250
touches; at ~2.5 touches per prospect on average, that's ~100 unique people).

These conversion rates are **blended, conservative estimates for highly
personalized, low-volume, multi-touch outreach from a brand-new sender.** They are
assumptions, not promises — track your real numbers and adjust.

| Stage | Conservative | Base case | Notes |
|-------|-------------|-----------|-------|
| Prospects sequenced | 100 | 100 | The full manual list |
| Any reply | 8% → 8 | 13% → 13 | Multi-touch + real personalization lifts this |
| Interested / positive | ~45% of replies → 4 | ~50% → 6–7 | The rest are "no" or "not now" |
| Calls booked | ~65% of interested → 3 | ~70% → 4–5 | Some interested never schedule |
| **Audits purchased** | **~60% of calls → 2** | **~65% → 3** | Guarantee + sample do the de-risking |

**Read of it:** the **base case lands at ~3 booked audits**, which is the goal.
The **conservative case lands at ~2** — meaning if your list or personalization is
weak, plan on **3–4 weeks**, not 2, to hit 3. The single biggest lever is list
quality (drag signals in `icp.md`) and the personalization slot actually being
specific. Blasting generic touches will underperform even the conservative column.

**What moves the numbers up:**
- Prioritizing the hottest signal — agencies *hiring a coordinator/admin* — into
  the first days of the sequence.
- Replying to any responder within the hour (booking rate drops fast after that).
- Referencing something genuinely specific in every first touch.

**What to do if week 1 underperforms:**
- If reply rate < ~6% by end of week 1, the problem is usually list fit or the
  first line — re-check drag signals and rewrite subject lines / hooks before
  adding volume.
- Don't fix a low reply rate by sending *more* generic touches; fix personalization
  and targeting first.

## Two-week snapshot

| | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|---|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| **Wk 1** | 25 (mostly first-touches) | 25 | 25 | 25 | 25 |
| **Wk 2** | 25 (follow-up heavy) | 25 | 25 | 25 | 25 |

By end of Week 1 you should have your first replies and ideally 1–2 calls booked;
Week 2 is where follow-ups mature and calls convert to purchased audits. Every
purchased audit is also a live shot at the $1,500 build — so the real 2-week win is
3 audits *and* 3 warm build conversations.

*(No client results are claimed anywhere in this plan — these are forward-looking
estimates to steer effort, clearly labeled as assumptions.)*
