# Cold Email Sequence — Recruiting Agencies

A 3-touch sequence: Day 0, Day 3, Day 7. Sent from {{FOUNDER_NAME}} at
Northstar Automation Co.

## Rules baked into these drafts
- Each email is **under 120 words** (body only).
- Each has **one** personalization slot — `[[...]]` — with a fill-in recipe you
  can complete in under 2 minutes from the prospect's LinkedIn/website.
- The **sample report** (fictional, clearly-labeled Harbor Lane demo) is linked in
  **touch 1 only**.
- The **guarantee** is stated **once**, in touch 1.
- **One** clear CTA per email: reply, or book a 20-minute call.
- Honest about being new. No invented clients, results, or testimonials. No fake
  scarcity, no "hope this finds you well."

Placeholders the founder fills before sending: `{{FOUNDER_NAME}}`, `{{CITY}}`,
`{{LANDING_PAGE_URL}}`, `{{SAMPLE_REPORT_LINK}}`, `{{CALENDAR_LINK}}`.

## Touch 1 — Day 0

**Subject:** the admin behind [[their vertical]] placements

**Personalization slot `[[their vertical]]`** — 2-min recipe: from their LinkedIn
headline or website, grab the niche they place for (e.g. "software engineering,"
"nursing," "finance"). Fall back to "your" if unclear.

**Body:**

Hi [First name],

I build automations for small recruiting agencies — the goal is to get recruiters
out of the admin (re-keying candidates between job boards and your ATS, formatting
submittals, chasing interview scheduling) and back on the phone.

I run a $400 AI Operations Audit: I map your workflow and send back a report
showing exactly what to automate, the hours and dollars it saves, and a 90-day
plan — in 48 hours. If it doesn't find at least 10 hours a month worth
recovering, it's free.

Here's a full sample (a fictional demo agency): {{SAMPLE_REPORT_LINK}}

Worth a 20-minute call to see if [[their vertical]] recruiting fits? Reply and I'll
send times.

{{FOUNDER_NAME}}

*(~105 words)*

## Touch 2 — Day 3

**Subject:** re: the admin behind [[their vertical]] placements

**Personalization slot `[[specific drag signal]]`** — 2-min recipe: from your
sheet's "Drag signal" column, name one concrete thing you saw — e.g. "you've got
9 roles open on LinkedIn right now" or "the coordinator role you're hiring for."
If you have no specific signal, use "juggling several open roles at once."

**Body:**

Hi [First name],

Quick follow-up. The reason I reached out specifically: [[specific drag signal]] —
that's usually where the manual re-keying and follow-up piles up in an agency your
size.

The audit just tells you where the biggest time leaks are and what a fix would
cost, so you can decide with numbers instead of guessing. No build required to
take the report and run with it yourself.

Open to a 20-minute call this week? Reply with a day that works and I'll send a
couple of times.

{{FOUNDER_NAME}}

*(~95 words)*

## Touch 3 — Day 7

**Subject:** re: the admin behind [[their vertical]] placements

month?

Either way, reply and let me know.

{{FOUNDER_NAME}}

## Notes for sending
- Send touches 2 and 3 as **replies on the same thread** (keep the subject with
  `re:`) so context stays intact.
- If they reply positively at any touch, stop the sequence and move to booking the
  call (see `call-script.md`).
- Send Tue–Thu mornings where possible (see `weekly-cadence.md`).
- Keep the sample link the same across all sends; it is the only proof asset and
  is clearly labeled fictional on its cover.
