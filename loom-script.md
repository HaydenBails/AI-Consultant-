# Loom Walkthrough Script — Sample Audit Report

**Runtime target:** ~2:00 · **Word count:** ~262 (with 2 marked pauses)
**Setup:** You on camera in the corner, screen-sharing `sample-audit-report.pdf`, flipping its 5 pages.
**Honesty note:** The on-screen report is the fictional Harbor Lane demo (property management). The script says so plainly, then promises the *format* — not a past client — is exactly what a recruiting agency gets.

`[PAUSE]` = stop talking, let the number sit on screen for ~2 seconds.

---

## SCRIPT (read aloud)

If you run a recruiting agency, you know the grind. Resumes pile up. Candidates and clients both want status updates. Your team spends half the day on triage and email instead of placing people. I built a four-hundred-dollar audit that shows you exactly where that time goes. Here's exactly what you get, page by page.

Quick, honest note first. The report on screen is a demo. Harbor Lane isn't a real company — I'm just launching, so this shows you the format, not a past client. Your version maps your recruiting workflows the same way.

Page two is the payoff. For this business, we found thirty-six hours a month. Over a thousand dollars, every month. **[PAUSE]** That's fourteen thousand a year, stuck in manual work — for one small team.

Everything's ranked by monthly value. Biggest win at the top, so you know where to start.

Take the top one. Forty intake requests a month, twenty minutes each to re-type and sort. That's thirteen hours, gone, every month — on one task. And I show you the math. No black box.

This next page matters most. It's what I tell you NOT to automate. The judgment calls. The sensitive conversations. If a machine shouldn't touch it, I say so. That's how you trust the rest.

Then a ninety-day plan. What to build first, second, and third. One thing at a time, so nothing breaks.

The offer's simple. Four hundred dollars. Two days. If I don't find you at least ten hours a month, you pay nothing. **[PAUSE]**

Want to see yours? Book a call with the link right here on this page.

## THREE ALTERNATIVE HOOKS (A/B test — each < 25 words)

1. **Your recruiters were hired to place people. Why do they spend half their day re-typing resumes and chasing status updates? Let's find those hours.** *(24)*

2. **How many hours a week does your agency lose to resume triage and status emails? My $400 audit puts a real number on it.** *(24)*

3. **If placing candidates is the job, why is your team buried in data entry and follow-up emails? Here's where the time actually goes.** *(23)*

---

### Every claim checked against a zero-client business
- No past client, result, or testimonial is claimed. The demo is named as fictional, on camera.
- All numbers spoken (36 hrs, $1,000+/mo, $14K/yr, 40 requests × 20 min = 13 hrs) are the labeled-illustrative Harbor Lane figures — presented as a demo, never as a real outcome.
- Offer terms ($400, 48 hours, 10+ hrs/month or free) are your actual terms and true on day one.
